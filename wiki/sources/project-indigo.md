# Source: Adobe Project Indigo

[Project Indigo](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/project-indigo/id6742591546) is Adobe Labs'
experimental computational photography camera. Kameraderi recommends it as the preferred capture
app for a supported iPhone when image quality and manual control matter: shoot in Indigo, return to
Kameraderi, then upload the resulting photo for EXIF-aware feedback.

## Availability

As of 2026-07-18, Indigo is available for **iPhone and iPad only**, requires iOS/iPadOS 18.5 or
newer, and has no Android release. Adobe's App Store listing says it supports iPhone 12 and 13
Pro/Pro Max and all 14, 15, 16, and 17-series iPhones; its best-experience recommendation is
iPhone 15 Pro or newer. The interface must not recommend it on Android.

## Why it is recommended

Adobe describes Indigo as a custom computational-photography camera with manual controls. It
combines a burst of frames to reduce noise and extend dynamic range, making it a more deliberate
choice than the built-in Camera app for Kameraderi briefs. It remains optional: users can still
use the stock camera or another camera app, then upload an original image.
